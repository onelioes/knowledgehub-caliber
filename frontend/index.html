What are the critical verification steps prior to starting up primary naphtha pump P-101A?
AI Copilot Verified SOP
Based on Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) and Maintenance Manual for Naphtha Pump P-101A at Chandra Asri Olefins Unit, the mandatory pre-startup checks are:

1. Lubrication Reservoir: Ensure lube oil level is at or above 65% with pressure gauge reading 2.1 bar.
2. Casing Venting: Open venting valve on pump casing until continuous liquid naphtha flow is established without air bubbles to prevent vapor lock.
3. Seal Flush (Plan 53A): Maintain barrier fluid pressure 1.5 bar above suction pressure.
4. Interlock System: Verify DCS safety permits and ensure cold/hot work permits have been signed off.

P-101A Critical (Tier 2)

Catastrophic Mechanical Seal Leakage (Primary Naphtha Pump)

Date: 14 August 2024
Downtime: 18h Unscheduled Shutdown
Root Cause: Clogging of Plan 53A flush orifice by fine coke particulates leading to dry running and SiC face thermal cracking.
Corrective Action: Installation of dual 25-micron duplex strainers and DCS automated differential pressure alarm.
K-102 Major (Tier 3)

Radial Bearing Vibration Spike & Interlock Trip (Syngas Compressor)

Date: 03 November 2024
Downtime: 6h Flaring Reduction
Root Cause: Polymer deposit buildup on stage 2 impeller due to cold recycle gas inducing 1X synchronous rotor imbalance.
Corrective Action: Reconfigured suction temperature setpoint 5°C above dew point and continuous anti-fouling flush.
F-101 Fully Mitigated

Radiant Coil Tube Metal Hotspot (Cracking Furnace)

Date: 19 January 2025
Downtime: Derating 15% (No Trip)
Root Cause: Local coke deposition caused by steam-to-oil ratio fluctuation during feed transition.
Corrective Action: Scheduled thermal steam-air decoking cycle updated to 60-day interval and recalibrated flow transmitters.
